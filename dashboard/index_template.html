🧭 Trade Compliance & Customs Analytics Control Tower

A simple dashboard for tracking customs compliance — one place to check paperwork, product classification, duty savings, and shipment risk.

Portfolio disclaimer: The product types shown are based on BASF's real, public business areas (from basf.com), just to make the demo feel realistic. Everything else here, including every company, deal, ship, amount, and tax rate, is made up for this demo and does not represent any real BASF customer, deal, or shipment. This project does not use or expose any real company data or systems, and it is not a substitute for an actual customs system (like SAP GTS). The duty and savings figures are just examples and would need to be checked by an expert before being used for anything real.
Built by Hani Ihsanuddin · Synthetic data, Python (pandas) · Medallion architecture (Bronze → Silver → Gold) · Visualized with Plotly.js
